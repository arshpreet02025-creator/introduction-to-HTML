Why your body need nutrients

Your body needs nutrients to provide energy, support growth, and run everyday chemical processes. Nutrients are split into different groups. Each group does a special job in your body. Major Nutrients and Their Jobs • Carbohydrates: These give your body quick and steady fuel. They keep your brain and muscles working. • Proteins: These build and fix your tissues. They help make muscles, skin, organs, and important body chemicals. • Fats: These store extra energy. They protect your organs and help your body absorb certain vitamins. • Vitamins: These tiny helpers protect your health. They boost your immune system and help chemical reactions happen. • Minerals: These build strong bones and teeth. They also keep your heart beating and control fluid balance.
